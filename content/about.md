Hi, I'm Jasmine.

I came to software the roundabout way — a Diploma of Information Technology at Monash College, which opened a pathway into a Bachelor of Computer Science at Monash University. I majored in Algorithms & Software, finished my coursework in July 2026, and graduate in October.

Most of my projects have started with a person rather than a problem statement: a ridesharing app built with three classmates for students at our university, a website for a family friend's home bakery so they can take orders online, and a website for a game I'm a fan of. I like the moment where something stops being a pile of code and turns into a thing someone can actually use. You can read about all of them on my [projects](/projects/) page.

I work mostly in web and Android development. Lately I've been curious about the layer underneath — how larger systems are designed so the pieces hold together, and how software reaches past the screen to talk to real machines. I'm slowly circling AI and automation too. That's not where my experience sits yet, but it's the direction I'm reading and building toward.

I'm based in Melbourne, happy to work in the CBD or the inner suburbs, and I can work in Australia without sponsorship. I'm looking for my first developer role and can start now.
