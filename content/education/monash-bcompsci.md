## Overview

I'm currently studying a Bachelor of Computer Science at Monash University, majoring in **Algorithms & Software** (previously known as Advanced Computer Science).

## What the major covers

This major focuses on the theoretical and practical foundations of software engineering — including algorithm design and analysis, data structures, software architecture, and applied problem-solving across a range of programming paradigms.

## Why I chose it

After completing the Diploma of Information Technology at Monash College, I wanted to deepen my understanding of computer science fundamentals and build the skills to design and ship real software — which led me to continue on to this degree.
