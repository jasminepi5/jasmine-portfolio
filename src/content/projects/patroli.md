## Background & Problem Statement

A business owner once came up to me with a specific problem they wanted to solve: to easily monitor if the security guards they hired had actually completed their assigned patrols.

## Solution (What is Patroli)?

Patroli is a web-application allowing users to assume 2 set of roles:

- Supervisor: Someone who checks what locations have been visited and who they've been visited by.
- Guard: Someone who patrols the perimeter of a location, and logs their visited assigned checkpoints.

It can be viewed as a website or installed as both a mobile and desktop application.
